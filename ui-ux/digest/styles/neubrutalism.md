# Neubrutalism

- id: `neubrutalism`  ·  tipo: General  ·  status: active
- pai: ``  ·  complexidade: Low
- era: 2020s Modern  ·  frameworks: tailwind/bootstrap
- light/dark: supported / supported  ·  perf: cost:low/drivers:none  ·  a11y: risk:low/requires:contrast-text-4.5,keyboard,visible-focus,reduced-motion

## Best for
Gen Z brands, startups, creative agencies, Figma-style apps, Notion-style interfaces, tech blogs

## Não usar para
Luxury brands, finance, healthcare, conservative industries (too playful)

## Cores primárias
#FFEB3B (Yellow), #FF5252 (Red), #2196F3 (Blue), #000000 (Black borders)

## Cores secundárias
Limited accent colors, high contrast combinations, no gradients allowed

## Efeitos & animação
box-shadow: 4px 4px 0 #000, border: 3px solid #000, no gradients, sharp corners (0px), bold typography

## Tokens
```css
--border-width: 3px, --shadow-offset: 4px, --shadow-color: #000, --colors: high saturation, --font: bold sans
```

## Checklist
Hard borders (2-4px), 
- Hard offset shadows, 
- High saturation colors, 
- Bold typography, 
- No blurs/gradients, 
- Distinctive 'ugly-cute' look

## Prompt de referência
Design a neubrutalist interface. Use: high contrast, hard black borders (3px+), bright pop colors, no blur, sharp or slightly rounded corners, bold typography, hard shadows (offset 4px 4px), raw aesthetic but functional.
