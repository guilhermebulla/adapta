# Sales Intelligence Dashboard

- id: `sales-intelligence-dashboard`  ·  tipo: BI/Analytics  ·  status: supplemental
- pai: `data-dense-dashboard`  ·  complexidade: Medium
- era: 2020s Modern  ·  frameworks: recharts/chartjs
- light/dark: supported / supported  ·  perf: cost:low/drivers:none  ·  a11y: risk:low/requires:contrast-text-4.5,keyboard,visible-focus,reduced-motion

## Best for
CRM dashboards, sales management, opportunity tracking, performance management, quota planning

## Não usar para
Marketing analytics, customer support metrics, HR dashboards

## Cores primárias
Sales colors: won (green), lost (red), in-progress (blue), blocked (orange), quota met (gold), quota missed (grey)

## Cores secundárias
Pipeline stage colors, rep performance colors, quota achievement colors, forecast accuracy colors

## Efeitos & animação
Deal movement animations, metric updates, leaderboard ranking changes, gauge needle movements, status change highlights

## Tokens
```css
--pipeline-colors: stage gradient, --gauge-track: #E5E7EB, --gauge-fill: primary, --rank-1-color: #FFD700, --rank-2-color: #C0C0C0, --rank-3-color: #CD7F32
```

## Checklist
Pipeline stages shown, 
- Deals draggable, 
- Quotas visualized, 
- Rankings updated, 
- Territory clickable, 
- CRM integration

## Prompt de referência
Design a sales intelligence dashboard. Use: pipeline funnel, deal cards (kanban), quota gauges, leaderboard table, territory map, win/loss ratios, forecast accuracy, activity timeline.
