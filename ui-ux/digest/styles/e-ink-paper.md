# E-Ink / Paper

- id: `e-ink-paper`  ·  tipo: General  ·  status: active
- pai: ``  ·  complexidade: Low
- era: 2020s Digital Well-being  ·  frameworks: tailwind/css
- light/dark: supported / not-recommended  ·  perf: cost:low/drivers:none  ·  a11y: risk:low/requires:contrast-text-4.5,keyboard,visible-focus,reduced-motion

## Best for
Reading apps, digital newspapers, minimal journals, distraction-free writing, slow-living brands

## Não usar para
Gaming, video platforms, high-energy marketing, dark mode dependent apps

## Cores primárias
Off-White #FDFBF7, Paper White #F5F5F5, Ink Black #1A1A1A

## Cores secundárias
Pencil Grey #4A4A4A, Highlighter Yellow #FFFF00 (accent)

## Efeitos & animação
No motion blur, distinct page turns, grain/noise texture, sharp transitions (no fade)

## Tokens
```css
--paper-bg: #FDFBF7, --ink-color: #1A1A1A, --pencil-grey: #4A4A4A, --border-color: #E0E0E0, --font-reading: Georgia, --transition: none
```

## Checklist
Paper background color, 
- High contrast text, 
- No animations, 
- Reading optimized, 
- Distraction-free, 
- Print-friendly

## Prompt de referência
Design an e-ink/paper style interface. Use: high contrast black on off-white, paper texture, no animations (instant transitions), reading-focused, minimal UI chrome, distraction-free, calm aesthetic, monochrome.
